Investor FAQs

Real Estate Financing Questions

Key considerations for commercial and residential real estate funding.

A Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) loan qualifies borrowers based on the property’s gross rental income compared to its principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and HOA dues (PITIA), rather than personal income tax returns or employment verification. If the rent covers the monthly debt obligations, the property qualifies.

For fix & flip projects, financing typically covers up to 85–90% of purchase price and 100% of construction costs (up to 75% of After-Repair Value). For stabilized commercial real estate and DSCR rentals, typical leverage ranges from 70% to 75% LTV depending on asset class and credit strength.

Yes, commercial and residential investment properties can be titled in an LLC, corporation, or partnership entity. Certain partner lenders in our nationwide network accommodate foreign national investor programs with appropriate US entity registration.
