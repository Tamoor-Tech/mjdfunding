Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial Business Loan FAQs

Answers to common questions about our corporate lending criteria and timeline.

Our middle-market programs generally look for companies with established annual gross revenues starting at $500,000+. For newer businesses or startups seeking sub-$500K facilities, we offer tailored small business working capital and credit lines through our specialized partner programs.

Collateral can include commercial real estate, corporate equipment and machinery, accounts receivable invoices, inventory, or a blanket lien on business assets. We also structure cash-flow based unsecured facilities for entities with exceptionally strong historical cash flow.

Initial pre-qualification takes between 24 and 48 hours. Depending on the complexity of the loan structure and required appraisals or legal review, funding can take between 7 and 21 business days.
