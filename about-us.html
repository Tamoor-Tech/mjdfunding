Our Foundation

A Modern Approach to Commercial Capital

At MJD Funding, we believe access to capital should be defined by transparent terms, rapid underwriting, and actionable partnership.

Securing commercial financing through conventional banks has become increasingly cumbersome, burdened by months of bureaucratic delay, rigid lending criteria, and surprise covenants. MJD Funding was created to eliminate those barriers.

By combining an extensive network of over 150 institutional lenders, private funds, and family offices with direct balance-sheet lending through MJD Capital Partners, we structure agile funding solutions that match each client’s operational timeline and growth trajectory.

Guiding Principles

Our Mission & Funding Philosophy

We operate under uncompromising standards of transparency, precision execution, and long-term fiduciary alignment.

Our Mission

To empower American business owners and real estate entrepreneurs by delivering frictionless, transparent, and sophisticated capital solutions that fuel expansion, create jobs, and build generational equity.

Our Vision

To be the premier capital facilitation partner nationwide, recognized for unrivaled institutional access, deal-structuring speed, and an unwavering commitment to client confidentiality and integrity.

Funding Philosophy

No hidden fees, no opaque terms. We evaluate every deal based on real-world asset value and cash flow potential, matching transactions with capital sources that offer optimal repayment flexibility.
