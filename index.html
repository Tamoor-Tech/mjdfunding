Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Funding Questions

Everything you need to know about our lending criteria, structuring timeline, and nationwide loan programs.

Before applying, clearly evaluate the exact use of proceeds, projected return on capital, your monthly cash flow debt service coverage, and your target repayment timeline. Having well-organized financial statements and a concise business summary significantly accelerates underwriter review.

Ensure all records are up to date: 6–12 months of corporate bank statements, recent tax returns, year-to-date profit & loss, and a schedule of existing liabilities. For real estate transactions, provide current rent rolls, purchase contracts, and appraisal materials if available.

Loan terms range from 6 months for short-term bridge or factoring facilities up to 25–30 years for permanent commercial real estate debt. Rates and amortizations are tailored to the collateral strength, borrower credit profile, and asset classification.

While our primary middle-market programs focus on established entities generating revenue, early-stage businesses can qualify for asset-backed equipment financing, accounts receivable lines, or direct lending opportunities through MJD Capital Partners if strong personal credit or collateral exists.

We maintain long-term relationships with our clients. Your dedicated MJD funding specialist remains available to support follow-on capital rounds, bridge refinances, credit line increases, or supplemental equipment acquisitions as your operational needs evolve.

Collateral requirements depend strictly on the loan program. Accounts receivable financing leverages unpaid invoices, equipment loans use the machinery as security, and real estate loans use the property. We also offer unsecured working capital facilities for highly qualified businesses with robust cash flows.
