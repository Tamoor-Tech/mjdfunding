Small Business Financing Structures

Multiple capital structures tailored to different operational stages, cash flow profiles, and credit situations.

Business Term Loans

$50K – $500K

Fixed repayment schedules with predictable monthly payments — ideal for businesses that need a one-time capital injection for a specific project or growth initiative.

  • Terms from 6 months to 7 years
  • Fixed monthly payments
  • Simple interest structures

Revolving Credit Lines

$25K – $250K

Flexible revolving capital that allows you to draw, repay, and redraw as operational needs evolve — interest accrues only on drawn balances.

  • Pay only on what you use
  • Ongoing access to capital
  • Good for seasonal cash flow

Business Credit Card Programs

0% Intro Periods

Access 0% introductory business credit card programs with 6–12 month interest-free periods. No minimum time in business required for select programs.

  • 0% intro APR available
  • Revolving purchasing power
  • Rewards and cash back options

Working Capital Advances

Fast Approval

Revenue-based advances repaid through a small daily or weekly percentage of business receipts — no fixed monthly obligations and highly flexible for revenue-fluctuating businesses.

  • Bad credit considered
  • Revenue-based repayment
  • As fast as 24–48 hours
